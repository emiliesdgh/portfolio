About

I am a robotics and computer science engineer based in Lausanne. I graduated from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) in October 2026 with a Master’s degree in Robotics and a minor in Computer Science.

I am interested in the creative side of technology: robots that move expressively, systems people can interact with, and machines that communicate. I enjoy challenging projects that combine robotics, machine learning, computer vision and art, and I like building them end to end, from the algorithm to the working prototype, together with designers, artists and engineers.

In my free time I paint, create, and spend a lot of time in museums and art exhibitions.

Projects

My projects span robotics, AI, computer graphics and interactive systems, each one an exploration of how machines can move, perceive and communicate. From aerial drones and industrial arms to wearable robots and virtual worlds, I build systems end to end: programming behaviour, designing interaction, and questioning what technology can express beyond function.

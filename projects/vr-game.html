Spring 2024 · Virtual Reality course

Virtual Reality Game

A fully playable Oculus Quest game: restore the elements by travelling through four themed worlds, using physics-based interactions.

  • EPFL, Lausanne
  • Oculus Quest
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Oculus Quest SDK
  • Interaction design
  • 3D environments

Concept

A stable, fully playable game for the Oculus Quest headset and controllers. It includes an in-game tutorial so new players can learn every mechanic on their own, and several scenes with different gameplay, physics-based interactions and environments designed specifically for VR.

Player interactions

Throwing

Physics-based throws: trajectory follows the speed and direction of the controller at release.

Containers

Place objects in a container, then pick up the container with its contents held in place by the physics.

Swing and hit

Hitting objects, such as a golf ball with a club, with force and direction taken from the swing.

Alternate grab

A custom grab-and-hold mechanic, distinct from the standard implementation.

Joint interaction

Doors and chests opened by grabbing and pulling, driven by physics joints rather than animation.

Teleportation

Point-and-select locomotion that moves the player quickly while limiting motion sickness.

Challenges

  • Keeping a steady 72+ FPS for a comfortable VR experience.
  • Realistic physics without causing motion sickness.
  • Interactions that feel natural without explanation.
  • A tutorial that teaches every mechanic without overwhelming the player.
